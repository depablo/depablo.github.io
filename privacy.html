Privacy policy

This policy covers the bapTV app (package com.bonsaponsi.baptv) for Android TV set-top boxes and Android phones.

No accounts, ads or tracking

bapTV has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics. Settings, channel lists and recordings stay on the device.

Network connections

  • Updates. The app asks the GitHub API (api.github.com) for the latest release and downloads it from GitHub. GitHub's privacy statement applies to these requests.
  • Internet channels and TV guides. Streams, playlists and XMLTV guides are loaded from the addresses that you add yourself.
  • HbbTV applications. Broadcasters' interactive applications started with the red button are loaded from the broadcaster's servers.
  • Phone remote. The remote works within your local network, between your phone and your set-top box.

Diagnostic reports

A report is sent only when you confirm it in the app (for example with Send report to the developer). It contains the app version, device model, TV inputs, the channel list and the app log. A short summary (app version, device model, error) becomes a public issue on GitHub; the full report is stored privately, used only to fix problems, and deleted after 180 days.

Permissions

  • Camera (phone app): only to scan the QR code shown on the TV to connect the remote.
  • Install packages: only to install bapTV updates that you accept.
  • TV and recording permissions (set-top box): to play, list and record channels from the tuner.
  • Accessibility service (set-top box, optional): only to receive the remote control's colour keys while a broadcaster's application is in front; it does not read screen content.
