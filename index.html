A free, open-source terrestrial TV (DVB-T/T2) player for Android TV set-top boxes, with a companion app for phones.

Download Source code Privacy policy

Features

  • Live TV and radio from the set-top box's built-in tuner
  • Channel list and management: reorder, hide, rename, categories
  • TV guide, programme information, audio and subtitle selection
  • Live pause, manual and scheduled recording
  • HbbTV applications (red button)
  • Internet channels (HLS, DASH) from M3U playlists
  • Phone remote, and a phone app for internet channels

Download

Each release has two files:

  • baptv-X.Y.Z.apk – for Android TV set-top boxes;
  • baptv-X.Y.Z-phone.apk – for Android phones.

Both are signed with the same key. Signing certificate SHA-256:
